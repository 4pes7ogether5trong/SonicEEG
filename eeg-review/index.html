Review from your iPad

  1. Open this app in Safari. Landscape gives the traces more room.
  2. Tap Open recording and choose files from the Files app. For BrainVision, select the header, data, and marker files together.
  3. Switch montages, page with the arrows or a horizontal swipe, and tap a trace for time and voltage.
  4. Use Safari’s Share menu → Add to Home Screen for convenient access. Internet is needed to load the app; offline installation is not included.

Recording formats

The File compatibility button below the viewer lists direct iPad readers, formats requiring the local computer converter, and native formats that still need a vendor export. Conversion is local; no patient files are uploaded.

What the display means

A division is the vertical distance between adjacent channel baselines. Amplitude is shown in µV/division; no physical millimeter calibration is assumed. The scale bar is authoritative at the current zoom. Negative voltages plot upward by default.

Longitudinal bipolar, transverse bipolar, average reference, averaged A1/A2, Cz reference, and custom electrode pairs use actual subtraction. Average reference includes the available, uniquely mapped 19 scalp positions and excludes ears and auxiliary channels. See Recording details for the exact contributors. T3/T4/T5/T6 are recognized as T7/T8/P7/P8. A1/A2 are not silently substituted with mastoids.

Remontaging requires the contributing channels to share a reference and sample rate. Unknown references require your confirmation in Recording details. Different known references, ambiguous duplicates, and missing electrodes remain unavailable.

Filters are causal second-order Butterworth high/low-pass and a Q=30 notch, with preceding data for settling. They can shift phase; early recording edges have limited settling. Filters already applied during acquisition cannot be reversed. Files remain in memory; only custom montage definitions are saved in this browser. Bookmarks disappear when you close or replace the recording unless exported.

This app does not upload EEG files or use analytics. The public app page can be opened by anyone; your selected files are not shared. Use de-identified data for initial validation. Touch performance and real-vendor compatibility still need acceptance testing.

Prepare a recording on your computer

  1. Download the local converter and unzip it on the computer holding your recording.
  2. Follow QUICKSTART.md to install its Python dependencies once, then launch the file-picker window. This is a Python utility, not a signed desktop installer.
  3. Choose the source recording and save a .eegreview file. Keep all original companion files together.
  4. Transfer that file using your approved file-transfer method. Open it here on your iPad.

Prepared files contain calibrated float64 voltage samples and metadata. The converter adds no filters, resampling, or rereferencing. Detected gaps, irregular timestamps, and unsupported variants stop conversion. Unknown references still require confirmation before changing reference.

Some non-voltage channels and separate event streams are omitted; the converter reports omissions. Full payload checksums can be verified with the converter; the iPad performs structural and sample-validity checks, not a full-file checksum scan.

Every reader remains an evaluation build. Fixture checks and a public example do not establish compatibility with every system version or clinical validity.
