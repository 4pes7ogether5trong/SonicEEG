LOCAL RECORDING

Bring your own signal

Open deidentified JSON, CSV or TSV. Samples stay in this browser. EDF, BDF, NPY and calibrated screen capture remain available through the local Python pipeline.

JSON: channel_names, sample_rate_hz, unit, data as channels × samples; null represents a gap. CSV: channel header, rows of samples, optional regular time column. Maximum 12 million samples and 80 MB.

MEASUREMENT GRAMMAR

Every change has a meaning

Rotate the signal field freely. Its positions and measurements do not change with the camera, age or playback speed.

Element Meaning
Channel position Fixed abstract bilateral layout. Not a head model or source localization.
Vertical position Frequency, logarithmic from 0.5 to 45 Hz.
Dot size Power density on a fixed logarithmic scale; clipped below −30 and above +30 dB. Exact values remain in the spectrum and export.
Dot color Frequency band; selected channel is white. Gray dots show reference A with a small display offset.
History view Selected channel: time across, frequency upward, power in depth. Gaps in time are preserved.
Band orchestra Delta 110, theta 220, alpha 440, beta 880, gamma 1760 Hz. Mean band power per hemisphere becomes acoustic gain via its square root.
Channel focus Pitch is measured peak frequency × 32; acoustic gain follows RMS voltage.
Raw trace Shared voltage scale. Pixel envelopes retain extrema; red marks show display clipping. DC removal subtracts the window mean.

RMS uses the mean-removed full window. Spectral metrics use a one-sided Hann periodogram over 0.5–45 Hz (limited by Nyquist). “Peak concentration” is the fraction near the largest spectral bin, not neuronal synchrony or a clinical confidence score.

Absolute sound and comparison need calibrated units. Missing samples invalidate that channel’s spectrum. Unrecognized labels receive an outer position and centered sound; no anatomy is inferred. Age is context only in the local pipeline.

The three recordings are illustrative synthetic mixtures: 25 channels, 512 Hz, 100 seconds each. Programmed BIRD-like and RMTD-like patterns are test targets, not expert-validated clinical exemplars. Interface trial results describe this exercise only.
